Writing about Faders?

Thank you. Everything you need is here, and you’re welcome to use it. For questions, review codes for Faders Plus or an interview, email support@faders.app.

In one line

Faders is a radio app for iPhone, iPad, CarPlay and Apple Watch that names every song, plays ad-free moods, and changes the channel when your station goes to a break.

In a paragraph

Faders plays tens of thousands of live radio stations from every country, with the song, artist and cover for what’s on, even when the station doesn’t say (Faders listens and identifies it on the device). When a station goes to an ad break, Faders can tune to another of your stations that’s playing a song and bring you back when the music returns. Like changing the channel by hand, it never alters the station’s stream. It also has ad-free Moods, a World Map, Car Mode, skins that turn the player into a turntable, cassette deck or mixing desk, Radio Loudness, Last.fm scrobbling, and your own music from Apple Music, Plex or Jellyfin. There is no account and no tracking.

Facts

  • Developer: Mayank Gulaty, an independent developer in Ireland
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, CarPlay, Apple Watch; made for iPhone Duo
  • First release: October 2026 (every update)
  • Price: free. Faders Plus is a one-time purchase (€4.99 launch price, €9.99 from 1 December 2026), shared with Family Sharing. No subscription.
  • Privacy: App Store privacy label “Data Not Collected”. No account, no analytics, no ads of ours.
  • App Store: apps.apple.com/app/id6825091695
  • Website: faders.app
