Looking for a TuneIn alternative on iPhone?

TuneIn is one of the biggest radio apps in the world, with over 100,000 stations, podcasts and, with its Premium subscription, live sports and audiobooks. If you mainly want live radio, though, you might want something simpler. Here’s an honest comparison.

At a glance

TuneInFaders
PriceFree, with a Premium subscriptionFree, with an optional one-time Plus purchase

Where Faders is different

  • No subscription. Listening is free. Faders Plus is a single purchase, shared with your family, for extras like pause and rewind, skins and alarms.
  • Song names, even when the station is silent. When a station doesn’t say what’s playing, Faders listens and identifies it, with the album, year and cover.
  • Changing the channel during breaks. Faders can move you to one of your stations playing a song while yours is on an ad break, and bring you back after. It never alters any station’s stream.
  • Private by design. No account and no tracking: the App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.
  • Ad-free moods from listener-supported stations, for focus, sleep and more.

When TuneIn is the better pick

If you want podcasts, audiobooks or US live sports coverage in the same app as your radio, TuneIn does all of that and Faders doesn’t. Faders is for people who want live radio, done really well.
