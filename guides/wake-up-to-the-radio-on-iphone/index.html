How to wake up to the radio on iPhone

The iPhone’s Clock app can play songs and tones, but not live radio. To wake up to your favourite station, you need a radio app with alarms.

With Faders

  1. Get Faders and favourite your station.
  2. Open Settings › Wake to Radio (part of Faders Plus).
  3. Pick a time, the days, and the station.

Faders uses a real iPhone alarm, so it rings even in silent mode and through Focus. Tap Listen on the alarm and Faders opens playing your station.

Tips for a gentle morning

  • Pick a breakfast show you like: a good presenter beats an alarm tone.
  • Use the sleep timer at night: fall asleep to a Sleep mood, and it fades out gently.
  • Calm mode (Faders Plus) turns your phone into a quiet bedside clock while the music plays.
  • Check your volume: the alarm uses your ringer volume.

Why live radio?

Weather, traffic and the news from your own area, plus the same voices every morning. That’s why clock radios were on bedside tables for 50 years.
