How to listen to live radio in CarPlay (and in cars without it)

Your car’s FM radio only reaches local stations, and DAB coverage comes and goes. Internet radio through your phone fixes both: any station, anywhere, as long as you have mobile data.

In a car with CarPlay

Any radio app that supports CarPlay appears on the car’s screen when you plug in or connect wirelessly. In Faders you get five tabs designed to be used at a glance:

  • Favorites: your stations, in your order
  • Recents: what you played last
  • Genres: when you fancy something different
  • Near You: local stations, wherever the road takes you
  • Your Music: your own library, if you’ve connected one

Tap a station and the Now Playing screen shows the song and cover. Next and previous move through your favourites.

In a car without CarPlay

Plenty of cars, including Teslas, don’t have CarPlay. Faders has a Car Mode for those: open it from the car icon on the Listen tab and you get big buttons and big type, made to be used in a phone mount, with sound through Bluetooth.

Before you drive

  • Pick your favourites at home. Searching while driving isn’t safe, and favourites are one tap away in CarPlay.
  • Data use: a typical station uses about 60 MB an hour at 128 kbps.
  • Siri works too: “Play Today FM on Faders” starts a station without touching anything.

Fewer ads on long drives

Turn on Change Station in Settings › Breaks. When your station goes to an ad break, Faders tunes to another of your favourites that’s playing a song, then brings you back when your station’s music returns, like you’d do with the dial, but without taking your eyes off the road.
