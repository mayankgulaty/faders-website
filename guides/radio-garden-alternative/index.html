Radio Garden alternative for iPhone

Radio Garden is a beautiful idea: spin a globe, tap a green dot, and hear the radio from that town. If you love it but want an everyday radio app as well, here’s how Faders compares.

What Radio Garden does well

  • The globe is a joy for discovering stations by place.
  • It’s free, with visual ads; a Premium purchase removes them and adds a sleep timer.
  • Favourites sync with iCloud, and the Lock Screen shows the song when the station sends it.

What Faders adds

  • A World Map too, plus search by name or frequency, genres in the languages you speak, and a country’s stations sorted by what’s popular.
  • Song names even when the station doesn’t send it, with album, year and cover, and a history of everything you heard.
  • Changing the channel during ad breaks to another of your stations playing music, then back. It never alters any stream.
  • Ad-free moods, CarPlay, Car Mode, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri and a sleep timer, free.
  • No ads of ours and no tracking. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Use both

They’re not either/or. Many people use the globe to discover a station, then add it to an everyday app for the car and the kitchen. In Faders you can also spin our globe on the website and land somewhere random.

Browse stations by country or by genre to find your next favourite.
