Christmas radio stations to stream online

From late November, radio stations around the world switch to Christmas songs, and some exist only for the season. Here’s where to find them, and how to listen on your iPhone, in the car or on a smart speaker.

Christmas FM (Ireland)

An Irish favourite: a charity station that comes on air every year for the season, playing Christmas music non-stop. It streams worldwide, so it’s a taste of home for Irish people abroad. Find Christmas FM Ireland and its sister channels for classical carols.

24/7 Christmas stations

Dozens of internet stations play Christmas music all season, and some all year: classic crooners, modern pop, carols, country and kids’ songs. Browse the most-listened Christmas stations.

Your usual station

Many pop and adult-contemporary stations switch to all-Christmas playlists in December. Check your favourites: in Faders their cards show what’s playing before you tune in.

The easy way: a Christmas shelf in Faders

From 20 November to 6 January, Faders adds a Christmas shelf to the Listen tab, with stations from your own country first. There’s gentle snow at the top of the screen, and a festive app icon if you’d like one. No update needed: it turns up by itself.

On a speaker

Use AirPlay to send any station from Faders to a HomePod, an AirPlay speaker or your TV, or connect any Bluetooth speaker.
