How to find out what song is playing on the radio

You’re in the car or the kitchen, a great song comes on, and the presenter doesn’t say what it was. Here’s how to find out, from the quickest to the most reliable.

1. Look at what the station sends

Most stations broadcast the title and artist alongside the audio, the same text you see on a car’s radio display. Any decent radio app shows it. The catch: plenty of stations send only their name or slogan, and some send the song a minute late.

2. Check the station’s playlist page

Many stations publish a “recently played” list on their website. It’s accurate, but it means stopping what you’re doing, finding the site and scrolling to the right time.

3. Use Music Recognition in Control Center

On iPhone you can add Music Recognition to Control Center (Settings › Control Center) and tap it while the song plays. It works for anything you can hear, but you have to catch the song while it’s still on, and it doesn’t know which station you were listening to.

4. Let your radio app do it for you

Faders combines the first and third: it shows what the station sends, and when the station doesn’t say, it listens to the stream itself and names the song, with the title, artist, album, year and cover, as each song starts. Every song you hear is saved to your history, so you can find the one from this morning later, and with Faders Plus one tap saves it to an Apple Music playlist (and loves it on Last.fm, if you’ve connected it).

It even works on your favourites before you tune in: their cards show what’s on right now.

When it can’t name a song

Song recognition needs a recording it knows. DJ mixes where songs blend into each other, live sessions, and presenters talking over the intro are the hardest. If one slips through, the station’s playlist page (tip 2) is your friend.
