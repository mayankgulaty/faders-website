How to listen to live radio on Apple Watch

Going for a run and leaving the phone at home? Your Apple Watch can play live radio on its own.

What you need

  • An Apple Watch with mobile data or Wi-Fi.
  • Bluetooth headphones paired with the Watch: the Watch speaker isn’t used for music.
  • A radio app with a Watch app, such as Faders.

Set up your stations

Favourite your stations in Faders on your iPhone and they appear on your Watch. On the Watch, tap a station to play it. If no headphones are connected, the Watch asks you to connect them.

Good to know

  • Data: streaming over mobile data uses about 60 MB an hour at 128 kbps.
  • Battery: streaming is heavier on the Watch battery than playing downloaded music.
  • Controls: pause and play from the Watch, as with any audio app.

With your iPhone nearby

When your iPhone is with you, it’s usually better to play on the phone and control it from the Watch: better battery, and every Faders feature, like song names and changing station during breaks.
