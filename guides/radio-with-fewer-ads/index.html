How to listen to the radio with fewer ads

Commercial radio pays for itself with ads, and that’s fair: they keep the station on air. But nobody tunes in for ten minutes of them. Here are honest ways to hear more music.

1. Listener-supported stations

Some of the best radio in the world has no ads at all, because listeners fund it: SomaFM, Radio Paradise and many community stations. In Faders these are the Moods: Focus, Chill, Sleep, Jazz Café, Energy and Retro, hand-picked and ad-free. If you enjoy one, consider supporting it directly.

2. Public broadcasters

Many public broadcasters carry few or no ads on their radio stations. Browse by country in our station directory.

3. Change the channel, automatically

This is what Faders was made for. Turn on Change Station in Settings › Breaks, and when your station goes to an ad break, Faders tunes to another of your favourite stations that’s playing a song. It keeps listening to your station and brings you back when its music returns.

It’s the same thing you’d do with a radio dial, just done for you. Faders never cuts, mutes or alters the station’s stream: the ads still play on the station, you’re simply listening to something else for a few minutes.

  • Stay leaves you on your station.
  • Change Station moves during ad breaks.
  • Music Only also moves when the presenter talks for a while.

You can choose differently for each station, so a talk station you love can stay as it is. It works with your phone locked too.

Is it perfect?

Not yet, and we’re honest about it: a break can take a few seconds to spot, and every station is different. If Faders gets one wrong, tap “It wasn’t a break” and it learns your station.
