How to listen to Irish radio abroad

Moved away, on holiday, or just missing the local station from home? Almost every Irish station streams online, so you can listen live from anywhere with an internet connection.

National stations

RTÉ Radio 1, RTÉ 2FM, RTÉ lyric fm, Today FM and Newstalk all stream worldwide.

Dublin and local stations

FM104, 98FM, Sunshine 106.8, Radio Nova and county stations from Cork to Donegal are all online too. Browse every popular Irish station.

The easiest way: one app for all of them

Instead of a different app for every broadcaster, Faders plays them all in one place:

  • Favourite the stations you grew up with, and see what’s on each before you tune in.
  • Every song named, even on stations that don’t send it.
  • Pause and rewind live radio with Faders Plus, handy when you’re in a different time zone and catch the end of something.
  • CarPlay, Apple Watch, widgets and Siri: “Play RTÉ Radio 1 on Faders”.

Will everything play abroad?

The live streams do. Now and then a single programme, often sport, may not be available outside Ireland because of broadcasting rights.

Time zones

Ireland is on GMT in winter and GMT+1 in summer. If the breakfast show is at 3 am where you are, try Wake to Radio in Faders Plus at a sensible hour, or rewind.
