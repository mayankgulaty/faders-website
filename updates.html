What’s new

Every update, newest first.

Faders is made by one person in Ireland and updated every week or two. Here’s everything that changed, in the words of the App Store notes. Get the latest on the App Store.

1.5 In App Review, November 2026

  • Breaks, rebuilt. In Settings › Breaks choose Stay, Change Station or Music Only (which also changes station when the presenter talks for a while), and pick a different choice for any station. Break checks now keep working with your phone locked, Faders comes back only once your station’s music has really returned, and “It wasn’t a break” teaches it your station.
  • Radio Loudness. Settings › Loudness evens out stations so a loud one doesn’t blast after a quiet one. Choose Match Apple Music, or 3, 6 or 9 dB quieter.
  • Last.fm (Faders Plus). Connect your Last.fm account and the songs you hear on the radio are scrobbled.
  • Reorder by dragging. Hold a station and drag it, in the list or the grid.
  • iPad and iPhone Duo. The cover sits right on the song and controls, and Search shows Around the World and World Map side by side.
  • Fixed: a rare crash when Faders went to the background while syncing with iCloud.

1.4 12 November 2026

  • Your Apple Music. Connect your library in Settings and play your albums, playlists and songs in Faders. Album pages take their cover’s colours.
  • A new look. The cover of the song playing fills the player and the whole app, softly blurred and slowly moving.
  • Save what you hear (Faders Plus). A bigger + in the player and a star on the Lock Screen save the song to your Favorite Songs or a Radio Finds playlist. The player now shows the album and the year too.
  • Songs on every card. Your favourites show what’s on even when a station doesn’t say, and more songs get their cover.
  • Smarter breaks. During a break, Faders moves to a favourite that’s playing a song right now, and comes back when your station’s music returns.
  • Longer rewind (Faders Plus). On stations that keep their own recording, go back hours, even before you tuned in.
  • Your stations as a grid. In Library, ⋯ › Show as Grid.
  • Fixed: For You no longer shows a station twice, and Faders opens properly on Mac.

1.3.1 November 2026

  • Songs on your stations. More of your favourites show the song that’s on, in Listen and in Library, and a song leaves the card when it ends.
  • Fixed: a phone call now pauses the radio, and it carries on when you hang up.

1.3 November 2026

  • Skins. Play the radio on a Turntable, a Cassette deck or a Mixing Desk, part of Faders Plus. Change station and the record changes, the tape reels spin up and slow down, and the desk’s meters follow the music.
  • Christmas Radio. From 20 November, a shelf of Christmas stations on Listen with stations from your own country first, gentle snow, and a festive app icon if you’d like one.
  • Livelier moods and genres. Every tile has its own little moving scene, and Moods follow the time of day.
  • Runs on iOS 26.1. Faders now installs on iPhones and iPads that aren’t on the latest iOS yet.
  • Fixed: the mini player could keep showing the previous station’s logo.

1.2 November 2026

  • Steadier streams. Many stations that used to stutter or repeat a few seconds now play smoothly, and a dropped connection picks up where it left off.
  • More stations. More local Irish stations, plus stations and their decade and genre spin-offs from Ireland, the UK, Scandinavia, Poland and Slovakia.
  • Downloads. Save albums and playlists from your Jellyfin or Plex server and play them with no connection.
  • Shuffle your favourites. One tap plays your favourite stations in a random order; Next moves on.
  • AirPlay. Choose a speaker from the mini player and Car Mode too.
  • Fixed: after pausing with AirPods, the next press starts the radio again.

1.1 October 2026

  • Your own music. Connect your Jellyfin server (or Plex) and play your library like radio, with a new Music tab to browse it.
  • World Map. Spin the globe and tune in anywhere.
  • Countries. Search a country’s stations, sorted by what’s popular now.
  • Car Mode. Big buttons for cars without CarPlay.

1.0.1 October 2026

  • An optional tip jar under Faders Plus, for anyone who’d like to support development. Tips unlock nothing; everything else stays the same.

1.0 October 2026

  • Faders arrives: tens of thousands of live stations, every song named, ad-free moods, CarPlay, Apple Watch, widgets and Siri.
