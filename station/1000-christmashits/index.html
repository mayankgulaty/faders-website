Questions

How can I listen to 1000 Christmashits on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for 1000 Christmashits and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to 1000 Christmashits in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, 1000 Christmashits plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on 1000 Christmashits?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When 1000 Christmashits doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
