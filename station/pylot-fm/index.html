Questions

How can I listen to Pylot FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Pylot FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Pylot FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Pylot FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Pylot FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Pylot FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
