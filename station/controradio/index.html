Questions

How can I listen to Controradio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Controradio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Controradio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Controradio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Controradio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Controradio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
