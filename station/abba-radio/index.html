Questions

How can I listen to ABBA Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ABBA Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ABBA Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ABBA Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ABBA Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ABBA Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
