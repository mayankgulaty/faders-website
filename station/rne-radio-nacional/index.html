Questions

How can I listen to RNE Radio Nacional on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for RNE Radio Nacional and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and RNE Radio Nacional starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to RNE Radio Nacional in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, RNE Radio Nacional plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on RNE Radio Nacional?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When RNE Radio Nacional doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
