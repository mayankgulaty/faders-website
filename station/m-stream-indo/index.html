Questions

How can I listen to M-Stream Indo on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for M-Stream Indo and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to M-Stream Indo in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, M-Stream Indo plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on M-Stream Indo?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When M-Stream Indo doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
