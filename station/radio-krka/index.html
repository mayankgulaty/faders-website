About Radio KRka

Radio KRka is a Slovenian radio station from Dolenjska, Slovenia playing balkan, mix, pop. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is radiokrka.si.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio KRka on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio KRka and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio KRka in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio KRka plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio KRka?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio KRka doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
