About Radio Ixchel

Radio Ixchel is a Spanish radio station from Sumpango, Guatemala playing community radio. It streams at 64 kbps AAC. Its own website is www.facebook.com.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Radio Ixchel. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Ixchel on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Ixchel and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Ixchel starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Ixchel in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Ixchel plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Ixchel?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Ixchel doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
