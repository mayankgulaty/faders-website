Questions

How can I listen to Positiva FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Positiva FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Positiva FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Positiva FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Positiva FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Positiva FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
