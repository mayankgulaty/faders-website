About Relax cafe

Relax cafe is an Estonian radio station from Estonia playing jazz, smooth jazz. It streams at 96 kbps AAC. Its own website is www.relaxfm.ee.

Questions

How can I listen to Relax cafe on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Relax cafe and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Relax cafe starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Relax cafe in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Relax cafe plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Relax cafe?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Relax cafe doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
