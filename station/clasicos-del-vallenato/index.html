Questions

How can I listen to Clasicos Del Vallenato on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Clasicos Del Vallenato and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Clasicos Del Vallenato starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Clasicos Del Vallenato in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Clasicos Del Vallenato plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Clasicos Del Vallenato?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Clasicos Del Vallenato doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
