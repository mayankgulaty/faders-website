About Balkan Radio

Balkan Radio is a Bulgarian radio station from Bulgaria playing america, balkan, balkansko. It streams at 128 kbps AAC+. Its own website is balkanradio.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Balkan Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Balkan Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Balkan Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Balkan Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Balkan Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Balkan Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Balkan Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
