How can I listen to Radio Sotenäs on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Sotenäs and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Sotenäs in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Sotenäs plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Sotenäs?
