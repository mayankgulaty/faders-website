Questions

How can I listen to Besame on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Besame and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Besame in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Besame plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Besame?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Besame doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
