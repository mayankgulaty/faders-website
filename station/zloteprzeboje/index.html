Questions

How can I listen to Zloteprzeboje on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Zloteprzeboje and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Zloteprzeboje in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Zloteprzeboje plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Zloteprzeboje?
