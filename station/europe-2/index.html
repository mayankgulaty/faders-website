Questions

How can I listen to Europe 2 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Europe 2 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Europe 2 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Europe 2 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Europe 2?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Europe 2 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
