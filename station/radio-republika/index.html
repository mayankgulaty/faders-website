Questions

How can I listen to Radio Republika on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Republika and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Republika in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Republika plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Republika?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Republika doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
