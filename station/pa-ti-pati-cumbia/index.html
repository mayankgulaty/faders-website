About Pa ti Pati cumbia

Pa ti Pati cumbia is an Español Internacional radio station from Ecuador playing cumbia, cumbia argentina, cumbia colombiana. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.lien.tv.

Yes. In Faders, Pa ti Pati cumbia plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.
