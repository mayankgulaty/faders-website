Questions

How can I listen to Tarab Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Tarab Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Tarab Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Tarab Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Tarab Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Tarab Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
