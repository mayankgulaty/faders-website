Questions

How can I listen to Raxies FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Raxies FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Raxies FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Raxies FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Raxies FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Raxies FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
