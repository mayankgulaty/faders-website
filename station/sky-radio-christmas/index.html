Questions

How can I listen to Sky Radio Christmas on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Sky Radio Christmas and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Sky Radio Christmas starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Sky Radio Christmas in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Sky Radio Christmas plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Sky Radio Christmas?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Sky Radio Christmas doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
