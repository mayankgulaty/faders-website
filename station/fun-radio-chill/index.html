About Fun Radio Chill

Fun Radio Chill is a Slovak radio station from Slovakia playing chill, chillout, jazz. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.funradio.sk.

Questions

How can I listen to Fun Radio Chill on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Fun Radio Chill and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Fun Radio Chill starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Fun Radio Chill in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Fun Radio Chill plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Fun Radio Chill?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Fun Radio Chill doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
