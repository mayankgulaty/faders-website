Questions

How can I listen to Naxi 80e on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Naxi 80e and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Naxi 80e starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Naxi 80e in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Naxi 80e plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Naxi 80e?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Naxi 80e doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
