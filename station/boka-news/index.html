About Boka News

Boka News is a radio station from Montenegro. It streams at 48 kbps AAC+. Its own website is bokanews.me.

Questions

How can I listen to Boka News on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Boka News and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Boka News starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Boka News in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Boka News plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Boka News?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Boka News doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
