About Jambo FM

Questions

How can I listen to Jambo FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Jambo FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Jambo FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Jambo FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Jambo FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Jambo FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
