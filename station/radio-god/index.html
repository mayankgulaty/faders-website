Questions

How can I listen to Radio GÖD on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio GÖD and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio GÖD in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio GÖD plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio GÖD?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio GÖD doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
