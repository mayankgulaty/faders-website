How can I listen to Cuban Flow Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Cuban Flow Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Cuban Flow Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Cuban Flow Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Cuban Flow Radio?
