Questions

How can I listen to Norsk Country Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Norsk Country Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Norsk Country Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Norsk Country Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Norsk Country Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Norsk Country Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
