Questions

How can I listen to Radio Tequila Oldies on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Tequila Oldies and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Tequila Oldies starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Tequila Oldies in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Tequila Oldies plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Tequila Oldies?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Tequila Oldies doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
