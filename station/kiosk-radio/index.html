Questions

How can I listen to Kiosk Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Kiosk Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Kiosk Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Kiosk Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Kiosk Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Kiosk Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
