Questions

How can I listen to Medi1 Hits on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Medi1 Hits and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Medi1 Hits starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Medi1 Hits in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Medi1 Hits plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Medi1 Hits?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Medi1 Hits doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
