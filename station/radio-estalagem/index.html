About Rádio Estalagem

Rádio Estalagem is a Portuguese radio station from Luanda, Angola playing talk. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is radio-estalagem.mdn.live.

Questions

How can I listen to Rádio Estalagem on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Rádio Estalagem and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Rádio Estalagem starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Rádio Estalagem in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Rádio Estalagem plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Rádio Estalagem?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Rádio Estalagem doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
