About Radio Sehara

Radio Sehara is a Bosnian radio station from Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina playing bosnia, folk, zabavna - narodna. It streams at 320 kbps AAC. Its own website is radiosehara.net.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Radio Sehara. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Sehara on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Sehara and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Sehara starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Sehara in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Sehara plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Sehara?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Sehara doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
