About Studio Digital

Studio Digital is an Espanish radio station from Lima, Peru playing cumbia, latino, música andina. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.facebook.com.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Studio Digital. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Studio Digital on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Studio Digital and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Studio Digital starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Studio Digital in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Studio Digital plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Studio Digital?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Studio Digital doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
