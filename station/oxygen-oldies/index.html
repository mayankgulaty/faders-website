Questions

How can I listen to Oxygen Oldies on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Oxygen Oldies and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Oxygen Oldies in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Oxygen Oldies plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Oxygen Oldies?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Oxygen Oldies doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
