Questions

How can I listen to Radyo Sputnik on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radyo Sputnik and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radyo Sputnik in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radyo Sputnik plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radyo Sputnik?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radyo Sputnik doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
