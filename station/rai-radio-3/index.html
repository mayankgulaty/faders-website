About Rai Radio 3

Rai Radio 3 is an Italian radio station from Lazio, Italy playing classical, culture, public radio. It streams at 320 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.radio3.rai.it.

Questions

How can I listen to Rai Radio 3 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Rai Radio 3 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Rai Radio 3 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Rai Radio 3 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Rai Radio 3 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Rai Radio 3?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Rai Radio 3 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
