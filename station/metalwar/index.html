Questions

How can I listen to Metalwar on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Metalwar and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Metalwar starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Metalwar in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Metalwar plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Metalwar?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Metalwar doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
