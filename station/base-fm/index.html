Questions

How can I listen to Base FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Base FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Base FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Base FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Base FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Base FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
