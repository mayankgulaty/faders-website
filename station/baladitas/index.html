Questions

How can I listen to Baladitas on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Baladitas and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Baladitas in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Baladitas plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Baladitas?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Baladitas doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
