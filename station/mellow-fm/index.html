Questions

How can I listen to Mellow FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Mellow FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Mellow FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Mellow FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Mellow FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Mellow FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Mellow FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
