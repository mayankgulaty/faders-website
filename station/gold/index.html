Questions

How can I listen to Gold on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Gold and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Gold in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Gold plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Gold?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Gold doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
