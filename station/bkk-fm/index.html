Questions

How can I listen to BKK.FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for BKK.FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and BKK.FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to BKK.FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, BKK.FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on BKK.FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When BKK.FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
