Questions

How can I listen to Dr.DIO Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Dr.DIO Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Dr.DIO Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Dr.DIO Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Dr.DIO Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Dr.DIO Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Dr.DIO Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
