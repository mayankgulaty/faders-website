Questions

How can I listen to Melody FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Melody FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Melody FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Melody FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Melody FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Melody FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
