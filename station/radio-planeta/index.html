Questions

How can I listen to Radio Planeta on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Planeta and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Planeta in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Planeta plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Planeta?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Planeta doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
