Questions

How can I listen to Al araby on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Al araby and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Al araby in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Al araby plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Al araby?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Al araby doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
