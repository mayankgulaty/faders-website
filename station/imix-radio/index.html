Questions

How can I listen to iMix Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for iMix Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to iMix Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, iMix Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on iMix Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When iMix Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
