Questions

How can I listen to U RADIO on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for U RADIO and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to U RADIO in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, U RADIO plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on U RADIO?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When U RADIO doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
