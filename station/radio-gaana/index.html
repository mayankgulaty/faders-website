Questions

How can I listen to Radio Gaana on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Gaana and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Gaana in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Gaana plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Gaana?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Gaana doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
