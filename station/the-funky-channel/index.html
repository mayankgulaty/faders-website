About The Funky Channel

The Funky Channel is a radio station from Belgium playing disco, funk, r&b. It streams at 192 kbps MP3. Its own website is thefunkychannel.com.

Questions

How can I listen to The Funky Channel on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for The Funky Channel and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to The Funky Channel in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, The Funky Channel plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on The Funky Channel?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When The Funky Channel doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
