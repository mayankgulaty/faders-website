How can I listen to Pop Radio 21 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Pop Radio 21 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Pop Radio 21 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Pop Radio 21 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Pop Radio 21?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Pop Radio 21 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
