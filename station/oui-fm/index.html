Questions

How can I listen to OUI FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for OUI FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and OUI FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to OUI FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, OUI FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on OUI FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When OUI FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
