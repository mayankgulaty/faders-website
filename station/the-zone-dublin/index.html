Questions

How can I listen to The Zone - Dublin on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for The Zone - Dublin and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and The Zone - Dublin starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to The Zone - Dublin in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, The Zone - Dublin plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on The Zone - Dublin?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When The Zone - Dublin doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
