Questions

How can I listen to Vikerraadio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Vikerraadio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Vikerraadio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Vikerraadio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Vikerraadio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Vikerraadio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
