About Radio Islam Malawi

Radio Islam Malawi is a Chichewa radio station from Limbe, Malawi playing education, islamic, news. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is radioislam.org.mw.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Radio Islam Malawi. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.
