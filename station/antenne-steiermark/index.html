Questions

How can I listen to Antenne Steiermark on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Antenne Steiermark and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Antenne Steiermark in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Antenne Steiermark plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Antenne Steiermark?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Antenne Steiermark doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
