Questions

How can I listen to Christmas Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Christmas Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Christmas Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Christmas Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Christmas Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Christmas Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
