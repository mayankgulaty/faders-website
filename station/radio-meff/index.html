Questions

How can I listen to Radio Meff on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Meff and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Meff in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Meff plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Meff?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Meff doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
