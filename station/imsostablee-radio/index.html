About Imsostablee Radio

Imsostablee Radio is a radio station from Israel playing alternative rock, dance, drum & bass. It streams at 320 kbps AAC. Its own website is www.imsostableeradio.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Imsostablee Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Imsostablee Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Imsostablee Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Imsostablee Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Imsostablee Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Imsostablee Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Imsostablee Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
