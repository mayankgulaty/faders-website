Questions

How can I listen to Romantic Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Romantic Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Romantic Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Romantic Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Romantic Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Romantic Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
