Questions

How can I listen to Irish Country Music Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Irish Country Music Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Irish Country Music Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Irish Country Music Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Irish Country Music Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Irish Country Music Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
