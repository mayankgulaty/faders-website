Questions

How can I listen to Radio Morava on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Morava and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Morava in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Morava plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Morava?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Morava doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
