Questions

How can I listen to Shakthi Fm on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Shakthi Fm and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Shakthi Fm starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Shakthi Fm in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Shakthi Fm plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Shakthi Fm?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Shakthi Fm doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
