About Super Clásica

Super Clásica is a Spanish radio station from Bogotá, Colombia playing 60s, 70s, 80s. It streams at 56 kbps MP3. Its own website is superclasica.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Super Clásica on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Super Clásica and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Super Clásica starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Super Clásica in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Super Clásica plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Super Clásica?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Super Clásica doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
