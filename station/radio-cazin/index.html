Questions

How can I listen to Radio Cazin on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Cazin and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Cazin in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Cazin plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Cazin?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Cazin doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
