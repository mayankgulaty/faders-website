Questions

How can I listen to SBS Love FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for SBS Love FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and SBS Love FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to SBS Love FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, SBS Love FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on SBS Love FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When SBS Love FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
