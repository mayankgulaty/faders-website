Questions

How can I listen to ABC NewsRadio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ABC NewsRadio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ABC NewsRadio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ABC NewsRadio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ABC NewsRadio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ABC NewsRadio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
