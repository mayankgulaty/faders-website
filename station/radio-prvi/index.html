About Radio Prvi

Radio Prvi is a Slovenian radio station from Slovenia playing news, public radio, rtvslo. It streams at 192 kbps AAC. Its own website is radioprvi.rtvslo.si.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Prvi on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Prvi and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Prvi in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Prvi plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Prvi?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Prvi doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
