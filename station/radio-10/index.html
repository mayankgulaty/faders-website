Questions

How can I listen to Radio 10 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio 10 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio 10 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio 10 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio 10 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio 10?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio 10 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
