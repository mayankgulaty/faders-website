Questions

How can I listen to DR P2 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for DR P2 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to DR P2 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, DR P2 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on DR P2?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When DR P2 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
