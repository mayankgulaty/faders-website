Questions

How can I listen to Neutral FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Neutral FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Neutral FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Neutral FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Neutral FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Neutral FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Neutral FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
