Questions

How can I listen to Súper Tokio Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Súper Tokio Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Súper Tokio Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Súper Tokio Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Súper Tokio Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Súper Tokio Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
