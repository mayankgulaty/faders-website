About Nova Wave Radio

Nova Wave Radio is a Spanish radio station from Puerto Rico playing caribbean, latin, latin jazz. It streams at 128 kbps AAC. Its own website is www.novawaveradio.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Nova Wave Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Nova Wave Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Nova Wave Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Nova Wave Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Nova Wave Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Nova Wave Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
