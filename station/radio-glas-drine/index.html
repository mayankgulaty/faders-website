About Radio Glas Drine

Radio Glas Drine is a Bosnian radio station from Bosnia and Herzegovina playing folk, narodna, zabavna. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is rtvglasdrine.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Glas Drine on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Glas Drine and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Glas Drine starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Glas Drine in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Glas Drine plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Glas Drine?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Glas Drine doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
