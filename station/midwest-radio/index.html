About Midwest Radio

Questions

How can I listen to Midwest Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Midwest Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Midwest Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Midwest Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Midwest Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Midwest Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Midwest Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
