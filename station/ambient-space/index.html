Questions

How can I listen to Ambient Space on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Ambient Space and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Ambient Space starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Ambient Space in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Ambient Space plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Ambient Space?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Ambient Space doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
