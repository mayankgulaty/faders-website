About Southside Stereo

Southside Stereo is an English radio station from Gauteng, South Africa playing afrobeats, amapiano, hip hop. Its own website is southsidestereo.co.za.

Questions

How can I listen to Southside Stereo on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Southside Stereo and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Southside Stereo in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Southside Stereo plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Southside Stereo?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Southside Stereo doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
