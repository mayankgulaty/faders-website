Questions

How can I listen to Remix FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Remix FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Remix FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Remix FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Remix FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Remix FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Remix FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
