Questions

How can I listen to Cats FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Cats FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Cats FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Cats FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Cats FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Cats FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Cats FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
