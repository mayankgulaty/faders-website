Questions

How can I listen to Rádio Sity on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Rádio Sity and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Rádio Sity in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Rádio Sity plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Rádio Sity?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Rádio Sity doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
