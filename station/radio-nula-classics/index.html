Questions

How can I listen to Radio Nula Classics on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Nula Classics and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Nula Classics in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Nula Classics plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Nula Classics?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Nula Classics doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
