About SLAY Radio [aac]

SLAY Radio [aac] is an English radio station from Sweden playing c64, electronic, remixes. It streams at 128 kbps AAC+. Its own website is www.slayradio.org.

Questions

How can I listen to SLAY Radio [aac] on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for SLAY Radio [aac] and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and SLAY Radio [aac] starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to SLAY Radio [aac] in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, SLAY Radio [aac] plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on SLAY Radio [aac]?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When SLAY Radio [aac] doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
