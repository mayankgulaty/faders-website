About Radio101

Questions

How can I listen to Radio101 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio101 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio101 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio101 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio101 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio101?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio101 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
