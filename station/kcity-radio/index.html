Questions

How can I listen to KCity Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for KCity Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to KCity Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, KCity Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on KCity Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When KCity Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
