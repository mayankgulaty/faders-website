Questions

How can I listen to Jazzy on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Jazzy and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Jazzy in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Jazzy plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Jazzy?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Jazzy doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
