Questions

How can I listen to Sky FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Sky FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Sky FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Sky FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Sky FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Sky FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
