About Alternative Radio

Alternative Radio is a radio station from France playing electro, folk, funk. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is alternativeradio.over-blog.com.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Alternative Radio. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Alternative Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Alternative Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Alternative Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Alternative Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Alternative Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Alternative Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Alternative Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
