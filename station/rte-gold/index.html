Questions

How can I listen to RTÉ Gold on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for RTÉ Gold and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to RTÉ Gold in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, RTÉ Gold plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on RTÉ Gold?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When RTÉ Gold doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
