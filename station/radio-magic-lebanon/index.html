Questions

How can I listen to Radio Magic Lebanon on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Magic Lebanon and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Magic Lebanon in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Magic Lebanon plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Magic Lebanon?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Magic Lebanon doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
