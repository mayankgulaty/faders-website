How can I listen to Sports Radio Brila FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Sports Radio Brila FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Sports Radio Brila FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Sports Radio Brila FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Sports Radio Brila FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Sports Radio Brila FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Sports Radio Brila FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
