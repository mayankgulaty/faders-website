Questions

How can I listen to NepalBodh on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for NepalBodh and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to NepalBodh in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, NepalBodh plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on NepalBodh?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When NepalBodh doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
