Questions

How can I listen to Dansbandskanalen on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Dansbandskanalen and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Dansbandskanalen starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Dansbandskanalen in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Dansbandskanalen plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Dansbandskanalen?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Dansbandskanalen doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
