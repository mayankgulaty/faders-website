Questions

How can I listen to Radio Bosanski Brod on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Bosanski Brod and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Bosanski Brod in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Bosanski Brod plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Bosanski Brod?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Bosanski Brod doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
