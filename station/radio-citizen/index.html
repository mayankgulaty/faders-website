About Radio Citizen

Radio Citizen is a Swahili radio station from Nairobi, Kenya playing news, swahili, talk. It streams at 130 kbps AAC. Its own website is citizen.digital.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Radio Citizen. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Citizen on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Citizen and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Citizen starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Citizen in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Citizen plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Citizen?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Citizen doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
