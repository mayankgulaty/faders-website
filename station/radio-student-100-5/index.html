How can I listen to Radio Student 100.5 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Student 100.5 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Student 100.5 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Student 100.5 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Student 100.5 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Student 100.5?
