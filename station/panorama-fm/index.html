Questions

How can I listen to Panorama FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Panorama FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Panorama FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Panorama FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Panorama FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Panorama FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
