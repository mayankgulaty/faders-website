Questions

How can I listen to Radio Audio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Audio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Audio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Audio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Audio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Audio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
