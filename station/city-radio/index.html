Questions

How can I listen to City Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for City Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to City Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, City Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on City Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When City Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
