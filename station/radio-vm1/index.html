Questions

How can I listen to Radio VM1 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio VM1 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio VM1 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio VM1 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio VM1 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio VM1?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio VM1 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
