Questions

How can I listen to Radio Relax on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Relax and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Relax in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Relax plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Relax?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Relax doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
