Questions

How can I listen to Raadio Kuku on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Raadio Kuku and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Raadio Kuku in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Raadio Kuku plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Raadio Kuku?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Raadio Kuku doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
