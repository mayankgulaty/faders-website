Questions

How can I listen to Chillhouse Live on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Chillhouse Live and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Chillhouse Live in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Chillhouse Live plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Chillhouse Live?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Chillhouse Live doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
