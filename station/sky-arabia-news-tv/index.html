Questions

How can I listen to Sky Arabia News TV on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Sky Arabia News TV and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Sky Arabia News TV in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Sky Arabia News TV plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Sky Arabia News TV?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Sky Arabia News TV doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
