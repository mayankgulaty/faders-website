About Deep in Radio

Deep in Radio is an English radio station from Lisbon, Portugal playing electro, house, trance. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.deepinradio.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Deep in Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Deep in Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Deep in Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Deep in Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Deep in Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Deep in Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Deep in Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
