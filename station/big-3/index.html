Questions

How can I listen to BIG 3 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for BIG 3 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to BIG 3 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, BIG 3 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on BIG 3?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When BIG 3 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
