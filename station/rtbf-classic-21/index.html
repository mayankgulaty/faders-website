Questions

How can I listen to RTBF Classic 21 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for RTBF Classic 21 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and RTBF Classic 21 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to RTBF Classic 21 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, RTBF Classic 21 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on RTBF Classic 21?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When RTBF Classic 21 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
