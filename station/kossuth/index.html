Questions

How can I listen to Kossuth on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Kossuth and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Kossuth in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Kossuth plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Kossuth?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Kossuth doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
