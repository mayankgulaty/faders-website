How can I listen to Slow English FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Slow English FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Slow English FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Slow English FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Slow English FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Slow English FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
