Questions

How can I listen to DR P4 Syd on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for DR P4 Syd and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to DR P4 Syd in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, DR P4 Syd plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on DR P4 Syd?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When DR P4 Syd doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
