Questions

How can I listen to Sunshine Live on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Sunshine Live and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Sunshine Live in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Sunshine Live plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Sunshine Live?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Sunshine Live doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
