Questions

How can I listen to Easy Instrumentals on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Easy Instrumentals and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Easy Instrumentals starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Easy Instrumentals in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Easy Instrumentals plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Easy Instrumentals?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Easy Instrumentals doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
