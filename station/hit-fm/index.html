Questions

How can I listen to Hit FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Hit FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Hit FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Hit FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Hit FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Hit FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
