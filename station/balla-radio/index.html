About Balla Radio

Balla Radio is an English radio station from Cameroon playing african music. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is ballaradio.com.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Balla Radio. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Balla Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Balla Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Balla Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Balla Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Balla Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Balla Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
