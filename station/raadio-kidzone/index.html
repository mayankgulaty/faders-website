About Raadio Kidzone

Raadio Kidzone is an English radio station from Estonia. It streams at 160 kbps AAC. Its own website is raadioduo.pleier.ee.

Questions

How can I listen to Raadio Kidzone on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Raadio Kidzone and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Raadio Kidzone starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Raadio Kidzone in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Raadio Kidzone plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Raadio Kidzone?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Raadio Kidzone doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
