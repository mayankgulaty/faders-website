Questions

How can I listen to Danmarks Radio P6 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Danmarks Radio P6 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Danmarks Radio P6 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Danmarks Radio P6 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Danmarks Radio P6 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Danmarks Radio P6?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Danmarks Radio P6 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
