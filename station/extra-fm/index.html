About Extra FM

Extra FM is a Croatian radio station from Grad Zagreb, Croatia playing folk. It streams at 96 kbps AAC+. Its own website is extrafm.hr.

Questions

How can I listen to Extra FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Extra FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Extra FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Extra FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Extra FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Extra FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
