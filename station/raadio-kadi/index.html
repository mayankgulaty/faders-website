Questions

How can I listen to Raadio Kadi on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Raadio Kadi and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Raadio Kadi in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Raadio Kadi plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Raadio Kadi?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Raadio Kadi doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
