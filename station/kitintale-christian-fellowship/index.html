How can I listen to Kitintale Christian Fellowship on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Kitintale Christian Fellowship and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Kitintale Christian Fellowship in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Kitintale Christian Fellowship plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.
