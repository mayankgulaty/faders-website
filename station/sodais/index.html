Questions

How can I listen to Sodais on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Sodais and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Sodais starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Sodais in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Sodais plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Sodais?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Sodais doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
