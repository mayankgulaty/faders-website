Questions

How can I listen to PlayFm Dublin on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for PlayFm Dublin and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and PlayFm Dublin starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to PlayFm Dublin in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, PlayFm Dublin plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on PlayFm Dublin?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When PlayFm Dublin doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
