Questions

How can I listen to Radio SWH Rock on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio SWH Rock and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio SWH Rock in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio SWH Rock plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio SWH Rock?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio SWH Rock doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
