About La Noventera

La Noventera is an Español - Latinoamerica radio station from Peru playing 90s, classic rock, música del recuerdo. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.facebook.com.

Questions

How can I listen to La Noventera on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for La Noventera and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to La Noventera in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, La Noventera plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on La Noventera?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When La Noventera doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
