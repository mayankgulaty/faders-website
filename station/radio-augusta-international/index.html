About Radio Augusta International

Radio Augusta International is an English radio station from Abidjan, Coted Ivoire playing 60s, 70s, oldies. It streams at 320 kbps MP3. Its own website is radioaugusta.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Augusta International on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Augusta International and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Augusta International starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Augusta International in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Augusta International plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Augusta International?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Augusta International doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
