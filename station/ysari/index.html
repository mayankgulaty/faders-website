About Ysäri

Questions

How can I listen to Ysäri on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Ysäri and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Ysäri in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Ysäri plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Ysäri?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Ysäri doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
