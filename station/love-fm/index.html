Questions

How can I listen to Love FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Love FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Love FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Love FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Love FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Love FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
