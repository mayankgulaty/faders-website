Questions

How can I listen to Oxygen Music on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Oxygen Music and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Oxygen Music starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Oxygen Music in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Oxygen Music plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Oxygen Music?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Oxygen Music doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
