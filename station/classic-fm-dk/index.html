Questions

How can I listen to Classic FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Classic FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Classic FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Classic FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Classic FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Classic FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
