Questions

How can I listen to Tip!k on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Tip!k and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Tip!k starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Tip!k in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Tip!k plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Tip!k?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Tip!k doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
