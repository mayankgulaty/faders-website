Questions

How can I listen to Adjud FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Adjud FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Adjud FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Adjud FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Adjud FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Adjud FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
