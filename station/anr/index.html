About ANR

ANR is a Danish radio station from Nordjylland, Denmark. It streams at 256 kbps AAC+. Its own website is anr.dk.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by ANR. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to ANR on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ANR and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and ANR starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ANR in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ANR plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ANR?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ANR doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
