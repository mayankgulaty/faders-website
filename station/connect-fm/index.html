About Connect Fm

Connect Fm is an Akan radio station from Ghana playing music, news, talk. It streams at 96 kbps MP3. Its own website is 3news.com.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Connect Fm. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Connect Fm on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Connect Fm and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Connect Fm in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Connect Fm plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Connect Fm?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Connect Fm doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
