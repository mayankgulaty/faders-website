About WaveMaster Radio

WaveMaster Radio is an English radio station from Lagune, Coted Ivoire playing afrobeat, hip hop, jazz. It streams at 192 kbps MP3. Its own website is wavemaster.diskstation.me.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by WaveMaster Radio. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to WaveMaster Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for WaveMaster Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and WaveMaster Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to WaveMaster Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, WaveMaster Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on WaveMaster Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When WaveMaster Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
