How can I listen to Mellow Bangla on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Mellow Bangla and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Mellow Bangla starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Mellow Bangla in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Mellow Bangla plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Mellow Bangla?
