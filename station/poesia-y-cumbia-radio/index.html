About Poesía y Cumbia Radio

Poesía y Cumbia Radio is a Spanish radio station from Santiago, Región Metropolitana, Chile playing 2000s, 90s, cumbia. It streams at 192 kbps MP3. Its own website is poesiaycumbia.cl.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Poesía y Cumbia Radio. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Poesía y Cumbia Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Poesía y Cumbia Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Poesía y Cumbia Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Poesía y Cumbia Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Poesía y Cumbia Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Poesía y Cumbia Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Poesía y Cumbia Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
