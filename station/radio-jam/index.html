About Radio Jam

Radio Jam is a French radio station from Coted Ivoire playing african music, folk music. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.radiojam.biz.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Radio Jam. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Jam on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Jam and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Jam in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Jam plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Jam?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Jam doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
