Questions

How can I listen to CLIAMP Synthwave Nights on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for CLIAMP Synthwave Nights and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to CLIAMP Synthwave Nights in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, CLIAMP Synthwave Nights plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on CLIAMP Synthwave Nights?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When CLIAMP Synthwave Nights doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
