Questions

How can I listen to ART RADIO on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ART RADIO and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ART RADIO in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ART RADIO plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ART RADIO?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ART RADIO doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
