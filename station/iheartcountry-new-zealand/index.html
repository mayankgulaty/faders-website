Questions

How can I listen to iHeartCountry New Zealand on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for iHeartCountry New Zealand and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to iHeartCountry New Zealand in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, iHeartCountry New Zealand plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on iHeartCountry New Zealand?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When iHeartCountry New Zealand doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
