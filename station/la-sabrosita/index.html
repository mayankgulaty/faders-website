Questions

How can I listen to La Sabrosita on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for La Sabrosita and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and La Sabrosita starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to La Sabrosita in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, La Sabrosita plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on La Sabrosita?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When La Sabrosita doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
