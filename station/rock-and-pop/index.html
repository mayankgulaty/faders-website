Questions

How can I listen to Rock and Pop on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Rock and Pop and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Rock and Pop starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Rock and Pop in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Rock and Pop plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Rock and Pop?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Rock and Pop doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
