About Restorer Radio

Restorer Radio is an English radio station from Ghana playing gospel. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is restorerradio.carrd.co.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Restorer Radio. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Restorer Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Restorer Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Restorer Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Restorer Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Restorer Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Restorer Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Restorer Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
