Questions

How can I listen to Radio Freccia on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Freccia and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Freccia in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Freccia plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Freccia?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Freccia doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
