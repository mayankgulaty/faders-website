Questions

How can I listen to Piratenpower on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Piratenpower and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Piratenpower in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Piratenpower plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Piratenpower?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Piratenpower doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
