Questions

How can I listen to Radio Commercial on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Commercial and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Commercial in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Commercial plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Commercial doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
