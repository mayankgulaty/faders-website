Questions

How can I listen to Techno.FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Techno.FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Techno.FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Techno.FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Techno.FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Techno.FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
