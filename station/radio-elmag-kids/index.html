Questions

How can I listen to Radio Elmag Kids on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Elmag Kids and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Elmag Kids in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Elmag Kids plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Elmag Kids?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Elmag Kids doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
