Questions

How can I listen to ZoyTV RADIO on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ZoyTV RADIO and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and ZoyTV RADIO starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ZoyTV RADIO in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ZoyTV RADIO plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ZoyTV RADIO?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ZoyTV RADIO doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
