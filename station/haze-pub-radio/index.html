About Haze Pub Radio

Haze Pub Radio is a Lithuanian radio station from Vilnius, Lithuania playing alternative, drum bass, electronic. It streams at 128 kbps OGG. Its own website is kalaver.in.

Questions

How can I listen to Haze Pub Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Haze Pub Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Haze Pub Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Haze Pub Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Haze Pub Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Haze Pub Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Haze Pub Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
