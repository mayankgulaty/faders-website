Questions

How can I listen to Funky Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Funky Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Funky Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Funky Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Funky Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Funky Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
