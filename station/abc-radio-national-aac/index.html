Questions

How can I listen to ABC Radio National AAC+ on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ABC Radio National AAC+ and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ABC Radio National AAC+ in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ABC Radio National AAC+ plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ABC Radio National AAC+?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ABC Radio National AAC+ doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
