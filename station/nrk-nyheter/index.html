Questions

How can I listen to NRK Nyheter on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for NRK Nyheter and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and NRK Nyheter starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to NRK Nyheter in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, NRK Nyheter plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on NRK Nyheter?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When NRK Nyheter doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
