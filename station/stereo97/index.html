Questions

How can I listen to Stereo97 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Stereo97 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Stereo97 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Stereo97 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Stereo97 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Stereo97?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Stereo97 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
