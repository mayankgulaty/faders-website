Questions

How can I listen to RÁDIO EXPRES on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for RÁDIO EXPRES and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and RÁDIO EXPRES starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to RÁDIO EXPRES in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, RÁDIO EXPRES plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on RÁDIO EXPRES?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When RÁDIO EXPRES doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
