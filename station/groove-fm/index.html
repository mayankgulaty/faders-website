Questions

How can I listen to Groove FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Groove FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Groove FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Groove FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Groove FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Groove FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
