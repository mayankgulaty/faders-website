Questions

How can I listen to neverland on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for neverland and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and neverland starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to neverland in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, neverland plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on neverland?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When neverland doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
