Questions

How can I listen to Fing Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Fing Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Fing Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Fing Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Fing Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Fing Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
