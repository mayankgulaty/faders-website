How can I listen to Newstalk ZB Wellington on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Newstalk ZB Wellington and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Newstalk ZB Wellington starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Newstalk ZB Wellington in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Newstalk ZB Wellington plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Newstalk ZB Wellington?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Newstalk ZB Wellington doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
