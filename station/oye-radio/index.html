About Oye Radio

Oye Radio is an English radio station from Queensland, Australia playing bollywood, desi, indian. It streams at 192 kbps MP3. Its own website is oye.radio.

Questions

How can I listen to Oye Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Oye Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Oye Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Oye Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Oye Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Oye Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Oye Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
