Questions

How can I listen to NPO Klassiek on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for NPO Klassiek and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to NPO Klassiek in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, NPO Klassiek plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on NPO Klassiek?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When NPO Klassiek doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
