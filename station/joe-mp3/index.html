Questions

How can I listen to JOE mp3 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for JOE mp3 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and JOE mp3 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to JOE mp3 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, JOE mp3 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on JOE mp3?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When JOE mp3 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
