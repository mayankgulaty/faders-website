Questions

How can I listen to Chillax FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Chillax FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Chillax FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Chillax FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Chillax FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Chillax FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
