Questions

How can I listen to Antena Krusevac on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Antena Krusevac and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Antena Krusevac starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Antena Krusevac in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Antena Krusevac plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Antena Krusevac?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Antena Krusevac doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
