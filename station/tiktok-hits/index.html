Questions

How can I listen to TikTok Hits on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for TikTok Hits and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to TikTok Hits in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, TikTok Hits plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on TikTok Hits?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When TikTok Hits doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
