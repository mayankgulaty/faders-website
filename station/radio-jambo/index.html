About Radio Jambo

Radio Jambo is a Swahili radio station from Nairobi, Kenya playing news, sports, talk. It streams at 63 kbps AAC. Its own website is www.radiojambo.co.ke.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Jambo on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Jambo and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Jambo in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Jambo plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Jambo?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Jambo doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
