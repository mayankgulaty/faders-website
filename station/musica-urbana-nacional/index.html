About Música Urbana Nacional

Música Urbana Nacional is an Español Peruano radio station from Lima Peru, Peru playing hip hop, rap hiphop rnb, reggaeton. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.musicaurbananacional.com.
