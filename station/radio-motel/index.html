Questions

How can I listen to Radio Motel on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Motel and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Motel in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Motel plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Motel?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Motel doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
