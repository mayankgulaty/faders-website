Questions

How can I listen to Classic Country Legends Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Classic Country Legends Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Classic Country Legends Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Classic Country Legends Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Classic Country Legends Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Classic Country Legends Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
