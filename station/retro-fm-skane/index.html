Questions

How can I listen to Retro FM Skåne on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Retro FM Skåne and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Retro FM Skåne in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Retro FM Skåne plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Retro FM Skåne?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Retro FM Skåne doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
