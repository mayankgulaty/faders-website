Questions

How can I listen to Classic21 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Classic21 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Classic21 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Classic21 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Classic21?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Classic21 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
