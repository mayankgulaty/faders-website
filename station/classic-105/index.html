About Classic 105

Classic 105 is an English radio station from Nairobi, Kenya playing classic rock, hits, pop. It streams at 192 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.classic105.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Classic 105 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Classic 105 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Classic 105 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Classic 105 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Classic 105 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Classic 105?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Classic 105 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
