Questions

How can I listen to Taylor Swift Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Taylor Swift Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Taylor Swift Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Taylor Swift Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Taylor Swift Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Taylor Swift Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
