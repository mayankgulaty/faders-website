About Skyrock Klassics

Skyrock Klassics is a French radio station from France continentale, France playing hip-hop and rap oldies. It streams at 128 kbps AAC. Its own website is skyrock.fm.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Skyrock Klassics. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Skyrock Klassics on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Skyrock Klassics and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Skyrock Klassics in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Skyrock Klassics plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Skyrock Klassics?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Skyrock Klassics doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
