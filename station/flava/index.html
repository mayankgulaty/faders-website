Questions

How can I listen to Flava on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Flava and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Flava in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Flava plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Flava?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Flava doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
