Questions

How can I listen to Fabulous 103 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Fabulous 103 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Fabulous 103 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Fabulous 103 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Fabulous 103 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Fabulous 103?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Fabulous 103 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
