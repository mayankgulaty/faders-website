Questions

How can I listen to International radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for International radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to International radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, International radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on International radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When International radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
