How can I listen to Magic Party Mix on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Magic Party Mix and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Magic Party Mix in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Magic Party Mix plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.
