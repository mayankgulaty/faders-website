Questions

How can I listen to ERR Klassikaraadio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ERR Klassikaraadio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and ERR Klassikaraadio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ERR Klassikaraadio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ERR Klassikaraadio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ERR Klassikaraadio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ERR Klassikaraadio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
