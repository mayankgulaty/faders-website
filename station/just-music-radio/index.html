Questions

How can I listen to Just Music Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Just Music Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Just Music Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Just Music Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Just Music Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Just Music Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
