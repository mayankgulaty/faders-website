Questions

How can I listen to bravo! on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for bravo! and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and bravo! starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to bravo! in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, bravo! plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on bravo!?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When bravo! doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
