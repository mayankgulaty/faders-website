About Radio Popolare

Radio Popolare is an Italian radio station from Italy playing independent, milano, news. It streams at 64 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.radiopopolare.it.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Popolare on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Popolare and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Popolare in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Popolare plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Popolare?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Popolare doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
