Questions

How can I listen to Lakhanda on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Lakhanda and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Lakhanda in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Lakhanda plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Lakhanda?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Lakhanda doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
