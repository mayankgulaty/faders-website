Questions

How can I listen to Zucca Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Zucca Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Zucca Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Zucca Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Zucca Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Zucca Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Zucca Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
