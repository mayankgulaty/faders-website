Questions

How can I listen to Signál Rádio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Signál Rádio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Signál Rádio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Signál Rádio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Signál Rádio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Signál Rádio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Signál Rádio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
