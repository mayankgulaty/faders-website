About Deep Radio

Questions

How can I listen to Deep Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Deep Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Deep Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Deep Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Deep Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Deep Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
