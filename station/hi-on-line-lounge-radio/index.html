Questions

How can I listen to Hi On Line Lounge Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Hi On Line Lounge Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Hi On Line Lounge Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Hi On Line Lounge Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Hi On Line Lounge Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Hi On Line Lounge Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
