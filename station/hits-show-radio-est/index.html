How can I listen to Hits Show Radio - EST on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Hits Show Radio - EST and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Hits Show Radio - EST starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Hits Show Radio - EST in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Hits Show Radio - EST plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Hits Show Radio - EST?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Hits Show Radio - EST doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
