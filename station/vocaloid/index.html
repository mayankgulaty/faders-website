Questions

How can I listen to Vocaloid on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Vocaloid and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Vocaloid in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Vocaloid plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Vocaloid?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Vocaloid doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
