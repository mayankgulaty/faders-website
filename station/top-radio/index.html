Questions

How can I listen to Top Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Top Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Top Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Top Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Top Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Top Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
