Questions

How can I listen to Radio Tunisie Culturelle on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Tunisie Culturelle and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Tunisie Culturelle starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Tunisie Culturelle in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Tunisie Culturelle plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Tunisie Culturelle?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Tunisie Culturelle doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
