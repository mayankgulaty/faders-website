Questions

How can I listen to Irie FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Irie FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Irie FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Irie FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Irie FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Irie FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Irie FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
