About Radio Folk Peru

Radio Folk Peru is a Spanish radio station from Peru playing andean, andino, folk. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.radiofolkperu.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Folk Peru on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Folk Peru and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Folk Peru in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Folk Peru plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Folk Peru?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Folk Peru doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
