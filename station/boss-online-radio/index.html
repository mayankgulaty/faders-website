Questions

How can I listen to Boss Online Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Boss Online Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Boss Online Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Boss Online Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Boss Online Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Boss Online Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
