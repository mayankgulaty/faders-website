About Classic Radio

Classic Radio is a Sinhala radio station from Sri Lanka. It streams at 64 kbps AAC. Its own website is trn.lk.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Classic Radio. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Classic Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Classic Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Classic Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Classic Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Classic Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Classic Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Classic Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
