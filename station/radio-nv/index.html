Questions

How can I listen to Radio NV on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio NV and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio NV starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio NV in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio NV plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio NV?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio NV doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
