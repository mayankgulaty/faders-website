Questions

How can I listen to Klara Continuo on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Klara Continuo and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Klara Continuo starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Klara Continuo in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Klara Continuo plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Klara Continuo?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Klara Continuo doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
