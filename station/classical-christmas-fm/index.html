Questions

How can I listen to Classical Christmas FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Classical Christmas FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Classical Christmas FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Classical Christmas FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Classical Christmas FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Classical Christmas FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
