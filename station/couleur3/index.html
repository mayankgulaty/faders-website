About Couleur3

Couleur3 is a French radio station from Lausanne, Switzerland playing indie, pop, rock. It streams at 96 kbps AAC. Its own website is programmesradio.rts.ch.

Questions

How can I listen to Couleur3 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Couleur3 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Couleur3 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Couleur3 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Couleur3 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Couleur3?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Couleur3 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
