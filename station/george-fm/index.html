Questions

How can I listen to George FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for George FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to George FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, George FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on George FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When George FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
