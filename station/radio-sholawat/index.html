About Radio Sholawat

Radio Sholawat is an Indonesian radio station from Surabaya, Indonesia playing religious, sholawat. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is temborocity.blogspot.com.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Radio Sholawat. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Sholawat on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Sholawat and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Sholawat in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Sholawat plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Sholawat?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Sholawat doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
