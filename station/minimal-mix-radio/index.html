Questions

How can I listen to Minimal Mix Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Minimal Mix Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Minimal Mix Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Minimal Mix Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Minimal Mix Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Minimal Mix Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
