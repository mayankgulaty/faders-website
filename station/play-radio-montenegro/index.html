Questions

How can I listen to Play Radio Montenegro on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Play Radio Montenegro and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Play Radio Montenegro in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Play Radio Montenegro plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Play Radio Montenegro?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Play Radio Montenegro doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
