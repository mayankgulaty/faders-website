Questions

How can I listen to Diab FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Diab FM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Diab FM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Diab FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Diab FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Diab FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Diab FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
