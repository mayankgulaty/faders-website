Questions

How can I listen to Criollisima GT on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Criollisima GT and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Criollisima GT in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Criollisima GT plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Criollisima GT?
