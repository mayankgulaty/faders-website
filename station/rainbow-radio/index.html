Questions

How can I listen to Rainbow Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Rainbow Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Rainbow Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Rainbow Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Rainbow Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Rainbow Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
