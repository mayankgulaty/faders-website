About La Diaria Radio

La Diaria Radio is a Spanish radio station from Uruguay playing cultural, news, talk radio. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is radio.ladiaria.com.uy.

Questions

How can I listen to La Diaria Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for La Diaria Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and La Diaria Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to La Diaria Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, La Diaria Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on La Diaria Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When La Diaria Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
