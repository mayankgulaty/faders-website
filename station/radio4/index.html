Questions

How can I listen to Radio4 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio4 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio4 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio4 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio4?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio4 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
