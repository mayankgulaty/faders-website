Questions

How can I listen to Radio 3 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio 3 and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio 3 starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio 3 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio 3 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio 3?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio 3 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
