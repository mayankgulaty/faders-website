About Radio Zürisee

Radio Zürisee is a German radio station from St. Gallen, Switzerland playing classic hits, classic rock, pop. It streams at 256 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.radiozuerisee.ch.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Zürisee on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Zürisee and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Zürisee starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Zürisee in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Zürisee plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Zürisee?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Zürisee doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
