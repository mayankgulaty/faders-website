Questions

How can I listen to Radio Fides Cochabamba on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Fides Cochabamba and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Fides Cochabamba starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Fides Cochabamba in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Fides Cochabamba plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Fides Cochabamba?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Fides Cochabamba doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
