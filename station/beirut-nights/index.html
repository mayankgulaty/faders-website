Questions

How can I listen to Beirut Nights on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Beirut Nights and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Beirut Nights in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Beirut Nights plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Beirut Nights?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Beirut Nights doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
