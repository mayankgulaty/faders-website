Questions

How can I listen to Star FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Star FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Star FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Star FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Star FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Star FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
