Questions

How can I listen to Mmuock Community Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Mmuock Community Radio and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Mmuock Community Radio starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Mmuock Community Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Mmuock Community Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Mmuock Community Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Mmuock Community Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
