Questions

How can I listen to Caribe Clásico on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Caribe Clásico and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Caribe Clásico in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Caribe Clásico plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Caribe Clásico?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Caribe Clásico doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
