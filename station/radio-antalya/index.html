About Radio Antalya

Radio Antalya is a Turkish radio station from Antalya, Türkiye playing downtempo, electronica, indie. It streams at 320 kbps AAC. Its own website is radioantalya.com.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Antalya on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Antalya and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Antalya in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Antalya plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Antalya?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Antalya doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
