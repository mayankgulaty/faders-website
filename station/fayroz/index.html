Questions

How can I listen to Fayroz on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Fayroz and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Fayroz in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Fayroz plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Fayroz?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Fayroz doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
