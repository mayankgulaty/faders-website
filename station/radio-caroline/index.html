Questions

How can I listen to Radio Caroline on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Caroline and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Caroline in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Caroline plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Caroline?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Caroline doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
