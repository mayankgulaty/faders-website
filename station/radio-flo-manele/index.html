Questions

How can I listen to Radio Flo Manele on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Flo Manele and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Flo Manele in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Flo Manele plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Flo Manele?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Flo Manele doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
