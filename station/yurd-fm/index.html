About Yurd FM

Yurd FM is an Azerbaijani radio station from Azerbaijan playing folk music. It streams at 192 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.yurdfm.az.

Questions

How can I listen to Yurd FM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Yurd FM and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Yurd FM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Yurd FM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Yurd FM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Yurd FM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
