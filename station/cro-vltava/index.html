Questions

How can I listen to ČRo Vltava on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ČRo Vltava and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ČRo Vltava in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ČRo Vltava plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ČRo Vltava?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ČRo Vltava doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
