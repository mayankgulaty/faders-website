Questions

How can I listen to Radio Sawt Alamal on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Sawt Alamal and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Sawt Alamal starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Sawt Alamal in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Sawt Alamal plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Sawt Alamal?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Sawt Alamal doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
