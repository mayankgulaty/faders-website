Questions

How can I listen to TripHop.Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for TripHop.Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to TripHop.Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, TripHop.Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on TripHop.Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When TripHop.Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
