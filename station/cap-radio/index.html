Questions

How can I listen to CAP RADIO on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for CAP RADIO and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to CAP RADIO in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, CAP RADIO plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.
