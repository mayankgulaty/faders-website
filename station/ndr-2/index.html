Questions

How can I listen to NDR 2 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for NDR 2 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to NDR 2 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, NDR 2 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on NDR 2?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When NDR 2 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
