Questions

How can I listen to Globe 360 Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Globe 360 Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Globe 360 Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Globe 360 Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Globe 360 Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Globe 360 Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
