About ABC Classic HLS

Questions

How can I listen to ABC Classic HLS on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ABC Classic HLS and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ABC Classic HLS in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ABC Classic HLS plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ABC Classic HLS?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ABC Classic HLS doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
