About Channel X

Channel X is a British English radio station from Auckland, New Zealand. It streams at 130 kbps AAC. Its own website is www.trb.co.nz.

Faders is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Channel X. The station’s name and logo belong to its owner.

Questions

How can I listen to Channel X on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Channel X and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Channel X starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Channel X in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Channel X plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Channel X?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Channel X doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
