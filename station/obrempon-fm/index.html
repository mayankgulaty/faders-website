How can I listen to Obrempon Fm on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Obrempon Fm and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Obrempon Fm starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Obrempon Fm in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Obrempon Fm plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Obrempon Fm?
