Questions

How can I listen to Rock 101 on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Rock 101 and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Rock 101 in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Rock 101 plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Rock 101?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Rock 101 doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
