Questions

How can I listen to ORF Radio Wien on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for ORF Radio Wien and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to ORF Radio Wien in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, ORF Radio Wien plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on ORF Radio Wien?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When ORF Radio Wien doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
