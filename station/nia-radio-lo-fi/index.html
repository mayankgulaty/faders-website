About NIA Radio Lo-Fi

Questions

How can I listen to NIA Radio Lo-Fi on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for NIA Radio Lo-Fi and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to NIA Radio Lo-Fi in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, NIA Radio Lo-Fi plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on NIA Radio Lo-Fi?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When NIA Radio Lo-Fi doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
