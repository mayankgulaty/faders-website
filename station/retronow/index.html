Questions

How can I listen to RetroNow on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for RetroNow and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to RetroNow in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, RetroNow plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on RetroNow?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When RetroNow doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
