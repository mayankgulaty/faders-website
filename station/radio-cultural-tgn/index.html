About Radio Cultural TGN

Radio Cultural TGN is a Spanish radio station from Guatemala playing christian-gospel, evangelical. It streams at 128 kbps MP3. Its own website is www.radiocultural.org.

Questions

How can I listen to Radio Cultural TGN on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Radio Cultural TGN and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Radio Cultural TGN starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Radio Cultural TGN in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Radio Cultural TGN plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Radio Cultural TGN?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Radio Cultural TGN doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
