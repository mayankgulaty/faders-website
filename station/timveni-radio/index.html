Questions

How can I listen to Timveni Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Timveni Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Timveni Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Timveni Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Timveni Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Timveni Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
