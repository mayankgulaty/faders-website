Questions

How can I listen to Extreme Deep House Radio on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Extreme Deep House Radio and tap it. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Extreme Deep House Radio in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Extreme Deep House Radio plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Extreme Deep House Radio?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Extreme Deep House Radio doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
