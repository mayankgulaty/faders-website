About PONdENDS.COM

Questions

How can I listen to PONdENDS.COM on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for PONdENDS.COM and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and PONdENDS.COM starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to PONdENDS.COM in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, PONdENDS.COM plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on PONdENDS.COM?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When PONdENDS.COM doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
