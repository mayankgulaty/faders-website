Questions

How can I listen to Talksport on iPhone?

Download Faders, free on the App Store, search for Talksport and tap it. Already have Faders? Tap Open in Faders on this page and Talksport starts playing. Add it to your favourites to see what’s on before you tune in.

Can I listen to Talksport in CarPlay?

Yes. In Faders, Talksport plays in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, and in Car Mode for cars without CarPlay.

What song is playing on Talksport?

Faders shows the song, artist and cover as each song starts. When Talksport doesn’t send it, Faders listens and names the song itself.
